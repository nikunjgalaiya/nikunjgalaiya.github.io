How I approach a problem

Understand.
Make explicit.
Check the evidence.

Start with the decision

Who needs to act, what do they need to know, and what would a useful outcome look like?

Define the terms and rules

Agree the scope, metric definitions, business rules and acceptance criteria before debating a dashboard.

Test the awkward cases

Include returns, zero demand, expired quotes, duplicate requests and competing stock reservations.

Explain what follows

Separate observed results from assumptions and identify the next decision that the evidence supports.
